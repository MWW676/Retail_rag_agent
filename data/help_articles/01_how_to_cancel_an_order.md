# How to cancel an order

Last updated: 2026-11-03

You can cancel an order as long as its status is **pending**. Once an order has moved past pending, it can no longer be cancelled.

## What we need from you

- The order ID of the order you want to cancel.
- The reason for cancelling. We can accept one of two reasons: **no longer needed** or **ordered by mistake**.

Before we cancel, we will read the details back to you and ask you to confirm with a clear "yes".

## What happens next

Your order status changes to **cancelled**, and the full amount is refunded to your original payment method:

- If you paid with a **gift card**, the refund is immediate.
- For any other payment method, the refund arrives in **5 to 7 business days**.

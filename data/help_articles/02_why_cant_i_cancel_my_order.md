# Why can't I cancel my order?

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Orders can only be cancelled while they are still **pending**. If your order has already started shipping, its status is no longer pending, so it can't be cancelled.

## Order statuses explained

- **Pending**: we have your order but have not started preparing it. You can still cancel it or make changes.
- **Processed**: your order is being prepared or is on its way. It can't be cancelled or changed at this stage.
- **Delivered**: your order has arrived. You can't cancel it, but you may be able to return or exchange items.
- **Cancelled**: the order has already been cancelled.

## What you can do instead

If your order is on its way, wait until it is delivered. Once the status shows **delivered**, you can request a return or an exchange.

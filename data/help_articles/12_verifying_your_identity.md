# Verifying your identity

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Before we can look up your account or make any changes, we need to confirm who you are. You can verify your identity with either:

- the **email address** on your account, or
- your **full name and zip code**.

We ask for this even if you already know your user ID.

For your privacy, we can only help with one account per conversation, and we can't share or change details on anyone else's account.

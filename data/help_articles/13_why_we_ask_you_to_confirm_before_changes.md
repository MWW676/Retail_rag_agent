# Why we ask you to confirm before changes

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Before we cancel, change, return, or exchange anything, we will list exactly what we are about to do and ask you to reply **"yes"** to confirm.

This protects you from changes you didn't intend. Some changes can't be undone. For example, changing the size or color of items on a pending order can only be done once, and the order can't be changed or cancelled afterwards.

If anything in the summary looks wrong, tell us before you confirm.

# Using gift cards

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Gift cards can be used in several places. The main rule is the same everywhere: the gift card must have enough balance to cover the amount it is paying for.

## Paying for an order with a gift card

If you change the payment method of a pending order to a gift card, the gift card must cover the **full order total**.

## Paying a price difference

When you change an item's size or color on a pending order, or exchange an item after delivery, the new item may cost more or less. If you use a gift card for the difference, it must have **enough balance to cover the price difference**.

## Refunds to a gift card

- When you cancel an order paid with a gift card, the refund to the gift card is **immediate**.
- When you return items, you can choose to receive the refund on an **existing gift card** instead of your original payment method.

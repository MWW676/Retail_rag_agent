# Exchanging items after delivery

Last updated: 2026-10-30

If your order has been **delivered**, you can exchange items for the same product in a different option, such as another size or color, if that option is available.

## Rules

- You can only exchange for the **same product**. Changing to a different type of product is not possible.
- Please tell us **all the items** you want to exchange at once.
- If the new item costs a different amount, provide a payment method to pay the difference or receive a refund. A gift card used for this must cover the difference.

## What happens next

After you confirm, your order status changes to **exchange requested**, and we will email you instructions on how to send the original items back. You don't need to place a new order.

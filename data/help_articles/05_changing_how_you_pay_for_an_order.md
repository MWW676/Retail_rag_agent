# Changing how you pay for an order

Last updated: 2026-11-05

You can change the payment method of an order while its status is **pending**.

## Rules

- You can switch to **one** payment method, and it must be different from the one you originally used.
- We accept three types of payment methods: **gift card**, **PayPal**, and **credit card**.
- If you switch to a gift card, it must have **enough balance to cover the full order total**.

## What happens next

We will confirm the change with you before updating it. Your order stays **pending**. The amount you originally paid is refunded:

- **Immediately**, if you originally paid with a gift card.
- Within **5 to 7 business days** for any other payment method.

# Changing size or color before your order ships

Last updated: 2026-11-12

If your order is still **pending**, you can swap an item for the same product in a different size, color, or other option, as long as that option is available.

## Things to know before you ask

- **You can only make this change once per order.** Please tell us every item you want to change in one go.
- After the change, your order status becomes **pending (items modified)**. From then on, the order **can't be changed or cancelled**.
- You can only switch to another option of the **same product**. For example, you can change a shirt from blue to red, but you can't change a shirt into a pair of shoes.
- If the new item costs a different amount, you will need to provide a payment method to pay the difference or receive a refund. A gift card used for this must cover the difference.

We will list all the changes and ask you to confirm before we make them.

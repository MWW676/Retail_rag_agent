# How to return items

Last updated: 2026-11-07

You can return items from an order once its status is **delivered**.

## What we need from you

- The order ID.
- The list of items you want to return.
- Where you would like the refund to go: your **original payment method** or an **existing gift card**.

We will read the details back to you and ask you to confirm before submitting the return.

## What happens next

Your order status changes to **return requested**, and we will email you instructions on how to send the items back.
